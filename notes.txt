WORKERS (N=1000-5000)
├─ Attributes: wage, skill, employment_status, grievance, risk_perception
├─ Behavior: work, consume, organize, protest if (grievance - risk) > threshold
└─ Interact: locally (proximity/network) + with firms

FIRMS (N=50-200)
├─ Attributes: productivity, capital, workforce, profit
├─ Behavior: hire/fire, set wages based on labor market tightness + profit
└─ Feedback: tight labor → raise wages; slack → cut wages or lay off

STATE (N=1, but endogenous decision-maker)
├─ Observe: Gini coefficient, unemployment rate, protest intensity
├─ Decide: fiscal policy (tax rate τ, redistribution R)
├─ Objective: maximize [social stability - redistribution cost]
└─ Constraint: budget balance (τ × wages ≈ R)
